# JMA Character Canon (from the user — authoritative)

- **Charlie the Polliwog** (aka Cpt. Charlie, Captain Charles P. Wog) — bandleader, lead singer, guitarist. Academy host. Voice: Sinatra / Louis Prima New York friendly wise guy. Catchphrase: "Jumpin' Jelly Beans!"
- **Finn Danger** — the shark (aka Finn, Finn the Shark, Sharky Finn, "Sharky"). Plays bass. **Finn and Sharky are ONE character** (the Note Match host "Sharky" is Finn). Voice: Jerry Lewis "HEY LADY!" — always on the brink of joyous laughter. Eager, literal, asks lots of questions, tells puns. Catchphrase: "Jumpin' jellyfish!"
- **Chunk** (aka Chunk E. Monk) — drummer. Voice: stereotypical surfer bro.
- **Jazzy the Jaguar** — upbeat, quick-witted band manager / sometimes trumpeter. Female. Loud, boisterous, fun-loving pun teller — cat puns ("purrrfect", "meow-sic", "kitten around"). "Jazzy's the name, and meow-sic's my game!"
- **Llama Lou** — non-verbal utility instrumentalist. Only "<llama talk>". Best friends with Stew, who often rides on his shoulder. **Never has voice lines.**
- **Stew** (aka Stew Kazoo) — DJ. In the show he communicates through kazoo sounds ("uh oh" in kazoo talk). Voice lines for Stew can be performed as kazoo-talk.
- **Dr. Jellybone** — smooth-talkin' jazzman, sax player. Velvet, gentlemanly, never loud. Running gag: "Dr. Jellybone prescription Fedoras."

Scripts: Jelly Music Academy (educational series) and The Jelly of the Month Club Show E1 (variety show) were shared as PDFs in June 2026.
